# ADR 005 Exact approval binding for mutations

Status: Adopted in blueprint v0.1; implementation pending.

Date: 5 October 2026.

## Context

An approved intention can diverge from a later tool payload, and timeouts can leave commit outcomes ambiguous.

## Decision

Prepare a canonical signed envelope containing principal, context, operation/version, targets, full payload, preconditions, expiry and one-use nonce. Approve its digest through a trusted UI. Commit consumes the prepared handle only, rechecks policy and concurrency, and never accepts replacement arguments. Unknown outcomes require reconciliation.

## Consequences

A hash alone is insufficient without authenticated approval binding. Exactly-once behavior requires appropriate upstream support. Disable actions that cannot satisfy necessary preconditions; do not promise atomicity across arbitrary services.

## Verification criteria

- Reject tampered payloads, stale versions, expired approvals and different principals.
- Reject nonce replay and simultaneous duplicate commits.
- Simulate timeout after upstream mutation and verify no blind retry.

## Alternative considered

Free-form model approval or confirmation of a summary without immutable payload binding; rejected.
