# ADR 011 Independent, inventory-bound ITEROP Business Process connector

Status: Adopted and implemented (NOVA-003, ITEROP first, 7 October 2026).

## Context

Olivier made ITEROP Business Process Play the only P0 application; Dataset Governance and AURA are postponed. ITEROP differs from the engineering-item service in origin, authentication (API Gateway with PFI, or an administrator-issued REST key), entitlement and schemas. The R2026x-FD04 Business Process API v2 OpenAPI is the specification of record.

## Decision

`server/iterop/` holds a dedicated connector:

- an `NOVA_ITEROP_*` configuration namespace;
- an operation inventory (`operations.ts`) for `getAllStartableProcesses`, `getTasksByUser` and `getBasicProcessInfo`, recording operationId, method, path, forbidden query parameters, allowlisted fields and scope;
- a contract schema that binds only those operationIds, with GET, read-only and principal-scoped. The contract cannot carry a path, only a tenant `basePath` and the reviewed field mapping;
- a synthetic adapter and a connection probe.

The operator, not the prompt, selects the ITEROP domain. Every upstream attempt is recorded as mission `provenance`: operationId, documented call, spec release, source, scope, outcome, coverage and time. The bounded GET transport lives in `server/http.ts` and is shared with the engineering gateway.

## Consequences

- A contract cannot redirect NOVA to an undocumented path or to a write operation. Adding a read operation means a reviewed inventory entry plus tests.
- Response schemas are pending the FD04 file. Until then, the field mapping is supplied only by the reviewed private contract.
- POST-based reads, pagination traversal, token exchange and all writes remain unimplemented.

## Verification criteria

`tests/iterop.test.ts` checks:

- the inventory;
- English and French routing;
- self-scope;
- zero calls in synthetic mode;
- that prompts cannot alter host, path, identity or scope;
- terminal 401/403 handling;
- redirect, malformed, oversize, schema and empty failure modes;
- contract rejection of writes, foreign operationIds and foreign documentation;
- that the template fails admission.

`tests/e2e/iterop.spec.ts` covers the UI journeys, accessibility, keyboard use and the absence of external requests.
